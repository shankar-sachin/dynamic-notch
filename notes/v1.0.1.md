A maintenance release, and the first one with a website.

The headline fix: a long album title could break the open panel. If a track's
title and album ran past about 580pt — *Kannum Kannum Kollaiyadithaal (Original
Motion Picture Soundtrack)* was the one that caught it — the text set its own
width, dragged the whole panel wider than the notch, and everything got clipped
against the shape: the artist line cut mid-word, the remaining-time counter, and
the last icon in the tab rail. Long text now truncates with an ellipsis and
cannot affect layout at all.

## Fixed

- **Long titles no longer break the player.** The title and artist lines had a
  minimum width equal to their whole string, so no stack above them could shrink
  them. They now truncate instead, and the panel stays the size of the notch
  whatever is playing.
- **Timer labels** coming from Apple's Clock are capped the same way.

## Added

- **The shelf survives quitting.** Files you leave in it are still there after a
  relaunch. Only paths are stored, never copies of your files, and anything
  moved or deleted in the meantime is dropped rather than left as an entry that
  won't open.
- **Paused music gives the notch back.** Pause for ten seconds and the pill
  shrinks to bare hardware instead of sitting in your menu bar all afternoon.
  The track stays in the panel, so it's waiting when you look — and pressing
  play reclaims the pill immediately.
- **A website**, at `docs/`, with a live notch you can hover.

## Changed

- Long text truncates rather than scrolling. The scrolling marquee is gone.
- `make dmg` and `make zip` build release artifacts, and the version on the
  website is stamped from `project.yml` so the two can't disagree.

## Installing

Download the `.dmg`, drag Dynamic Notch to Applications, and open it. There's no
Dock icon — look in the menu bar, and look up.

**macOS will refuse to open it the first time.** This app isn't notarised, which
needs a paid Apple Developer account, so Gatekeeper has nothing to check it
against — every unnotarised app gets the same dialog. Go to **System Settings →
Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**, then open it again. Or,
from Terminal:

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Dynamic Notch.app"
```

Requires macOS 26 or later.
